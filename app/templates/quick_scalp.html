What this is: rebuilt 8 Sep 2026 -- same VWAP 2σ mean-reversion signal as the 4 Sep build, on a genuinely real-time engine and a tightened risk construction. Each 1-minute bar is now finalized the instant a live tick's minute rolls over (a persistent WebSocket feed, not a periodic REST poll), then checked against a session VWAP and its 2σ bands: a bar that pierces a band, closes back inside it, leaves a rejection wick covering ≥30% of its range, and has RSI(7) confirming exhaustion (<30 on a lower-band piercing, >70 on an upper one) arms a setup. If the very next completed bar trades back through that setup bar's opposite extreme, that's the entry trigger -- a Deep ITM contract (~100 points from spot, opposite direction from a plain ATM pick) is bought as ONE single-clip position, no more Target-1/Runner leg split.

Risk, checked every cycle: a flat -2.5% option-premium stop enforced by the same 30-second backstop every strategy here uses; a structural index-level stop (the setup bar's own rejection extreme ±1 point, capped at 14 points from the trigger price) checked independently against the live spot; and at the 3-minute mark, a branch on P&L -- already profitable enough to cover round-trip costs (premium ≥ entry + ₹2) gets its stop trailed to that breakeven-plus-buffer level and is left open to run toward the real +3.5-4% target (floored at +₹12), while a still-flat/negative position is scratched immediately. The old build force-closed everything at 3 minutes regardless of P&L -- that's what clipped two same-day trades at a net loss despite a small favorable move, part of what prompted this rebuild.

What this is not: validated. The entry SIGNAL now runs on real ticks, but position MONITORING (stop/target/structural/time-stop) still runs on a fast 5-second poll rather than a second, per-position WebSocket channel -- deliberately, matching this project's own Validated Signal precedent for the same tradeoff. Adding a second persistent WebSocket connection is also a real, material resource cost on a box this project's own notes already describe as memory-constrained -- if capacity pressure worsens, this feed (like AI Origination before it) is a specific, known thing to consider pausing. Paper trading only -- no live-order code path exists for this strategy, so "hard broker-level SL-M" is a fast, deterministic paper stop, not a real exchange order.
