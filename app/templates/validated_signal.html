What this is (rebuilt 5 Sep 2026): a fully deterministic Morning & Afternoon breakout engine, built to an external specification exactly as written. Two independent windows: a Morning Trend Impulse (09:25-10:45 IST) breaking out of max(previous-day high, opening range high) / the mirrored low, and an Afternoon Box Expansion (13:15-14:30 IST) breaking out of an 11:00-13:15 consolidation box -- both gated on a volume surge over a 20-bar average and a hard per-trade risk cap, both sized at a strict 1:2 risk:reward. Every setup, trigger, stop and target is computed on 5-minute SPOT INDEX candles only -- the option is purely the execution vehicle, never the source of any indicator. At most one position is open across Nifty and Bank Nifty combined; if both trigger in the same 5-minute cycle, the one with the higher trigger-candle volume (relative to its own 20-bar average) wins. Contracts are 1-strike In-The-Money, not ATM. Runs on its own two scheduler jobs -- a 5-minute entry check and a genuinely new 5-second exit poll, the fastest job in this app -- fully isolated from every other trade under its own origin.

The stop/target/stagnation exit engine is SPOT-based, not premium-based, unlike every other strategy on this dashboard. The "Stop"/"Target" figures below are real INDEX price levels checked every 5 seconds against live spot LTP -- not the option's own premium. A trade also exits automatically if it hasn't moved at least 1x its own entry risk within 20 minutes (a stagnation cutoff), or at a hard session cutoff (11:15 IST for a morning trade, 15:10 IST for an afternoon one) regardless of where price sits.

What this is not: a validated strategy, or a promise of a win. Every threshold here (buffers, risk caps, box widths, the volume multipliers, the 20-minute stagnation window) is the external spec's own number, not something this project's own backtesting has produced or confirmed. Every number below is a live, ongoing measurement with no minimum sample size met yet. Paper trading only -- no live-order code path exists for this strategy.
